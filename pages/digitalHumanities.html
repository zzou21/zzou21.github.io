Digital Humanities (DH) Projects

I combine my history research with computational methodologies and tools. The ultimate goal of digital humanities is not to create some sort of "super scholar" who has a PhD in history and PhD in computer science (although I won't be opposed to that). It is, instead, to build a mutually respectful and humble intellectual space where people from different disciplines actually listen to each other and try to see problems from other disciplines' perspectives rather than just wait for people from other disciplines to finish talking.

My DH Background

For my DH work, I currently focus on Natural Language Processing (esp. named entity recognition) and open-source software development. Los Angeles's Oil Industry

2024 - now

My research on the oil industry in Los Angeles (and California) has multiple parts, including a digital one. There are multiple sections to my digital humanities work on LA's oil industry. They are:

  1. An API pipeline to connect to Library of Congress's Chronicling America database designed to collect newspaper pages that mention the LA oil industry.
  2. An animated graph of newspaper coverage of LA oil between 1890 and 1930 in the entire US.
  3. Derived quantitative data from the US Census Bureau and US Geographic Names Information System to match unstandardized newspaper publication cities as metadata in Library of Congress to cartographic locations to display them on maps.
